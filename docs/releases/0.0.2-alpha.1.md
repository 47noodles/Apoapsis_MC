# Apoapsis 0.0.2-alpha.1 — "Stage 0 prototype"

*Unreleased (branch `stage0-prototype`) · Minecraft 26.3 · NeoForge 26.3.0.69-beta*

## Highlights

The first playable Apoapsis. Build a rocket block by block, fly it, climb into orbit, and burn for Pale, a dry moon with a lighter pull to land on. The art and menus are placeholders. The flying is the real core: your ship's mass, thrust and fuel decide how it moves, and orbits follow Kepler's laws.

## New
- **Build a ship.** Hull plates, cockpits, gyroscopes, fuel tanks, Spark thrusters and hydrolox canisters. Right-click a cockpit to board, and the blocks become one ship.
- **Fly it.** W and S tip the nose forward and back. A and D turn it, and Q and E roll it. R and F set the throttle, Z goes to full and X cuts it. G holds the ship's attitude (SAS).
- **Reach orbit.** Climb out of Verdance's air and into orbit, where the sky is full of stars.
- **Burn for Pale.** Press M for the full-screen map of the system. In orbit, wait for the transfer window, burn about 190 m/s prograde, and coast in. Pale's gravity takes over at its edge, and you can land there at 0.16 g.
- **Ages.** Ship parts unlock as you advance. You start in Age 0, Landfall. Hold a copper ingot for Age 1 (Hearth), an iron ingot for Age 2 (Steam and Brass), and a piston for Age 3 (Current), which unlocks the hull plate, cockpit and gyroscope. Fly a ship above 1,500 m (*First Flight*) for Age 4 (Ascent), which unlocks the fuel tank, Spark thruster and hydrolox canister.
- **Test save.** For testers: `./gradlew runTestFlight` (see the README) opens *Apoapsis Test Flight*, with a Kestrel rocket already in orbit and a second one on a pad at spawn.
- **Commands.** `/apoapsis scenario pad` builds a fuelled Kestrel next to you and raises you to Age 4. `/apoapsis scenario orbit` puts a Kestrel in a 16 km orbit with you in the cockpit.

## Balance
- Hull plate: 400 kg. Cockpit: 800 kg. Gyroscope: 500 kg, with 200 kN·m of torque.
- Fuel tank: 150 kg empty, holds 1,200 kg of hydrolox. Hydrolox canister: adds 300 kg.
- Spark thruster: 350 kg, with 100 kN of thrust in vacuum. It is more fuel-efficient in vacuum (440 s) than at sea level (360 s).
- Verdance: 64 km radius, with air up to 12 km. Pale: 20 km radius, 0.16 g, no air.
- A circular orbit 15 km up takes about 710 m/s. The transfer burn to Pale is about 190 m/s.

## For server owners
- Time warp works only in singleplayer. This build is not aimed at servers yet, so expect rough edges there.

## Upgrading
- Back up your world before updating.
- There is no earlier game build to upgrade from, so there is no save migration.

## For addon authors
- The orbit library (`orbit/`, pure Java) ships inside the mod jar. Everything is alpha, and any API may change in any release.

## Known issues
- Flat worlds only. Round planets come in Stage 3.
- Simple physics. Ship physics gets more realistic in later stages.
- No spacewalks: you cannot leave a ship in orbit.
- Placeholder art and UI throughout.
- Time warp works only in singleplayer.
